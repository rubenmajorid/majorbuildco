Meet the founder

Built from the ground up.
Built to last.

Ruben Castaños is the founder of Major Building & Development LLC, an Idaho-based construction company focused on quality craftsmanship, dependable project management and projects made to last.

Ruben built his experience from the ground up, working hands-on in residential construction with a strong background in framing and custom homes. That field experience means he knows not only how a project should look when it's finished, but how it needs to be built from the very beginning.

As a licensed general contractor, he works closely with homeowners, subcontractors, designers and other professionals to keep every project organized and moving forward — from framing and custom homes to remodels, tenant improvements and project management.

Whether managing a project or working in the field, Ruben takes ownership of the work, solves problems as they come up, and treats every project as a reflection of the Major name.
